Announcement August 17, 2026 Interest Notification for X12 ANS Proposal Periodically X12 proposes a new American National Standard (ANS) for approval. X12 member representatives and individual non-members are both eligible to participate in the consensus process by registering their interest in being part of the consensus body that will vote on the proposal. At this time, X12 is forming the consensus body that will vote on the Version 009000 proposal.
